Dashboard

A personal home page app, used only by its developer.

What it does

Dashboard is a small private web page that runs on its developer's own computer. It shows the day at a glance: a clock, the day's calendar events, a to-do list, and a few personal notes.

It connects to Google with read-only access so it can show:

  • Google Tasks: the developer's own task lists and tasks (read-only).
  • Google Calendar: the title, time and location of the developer's own events (read-only).

Dashboard cannot create, change or delete anything in Google. Tasks and events are edited in Google's own apps; Dashboard only displays them.

Who it's for

Dashboard is not offered to the public. It is used by one person, its developer, with their own Google account.
